## North Star

> Booked-out regenerative aesthetics practice in Stoneham with a waitlist, then a multi-provider wellness flagship.

## Diagnosis

Core problem is **demand recovery + brand confidence + capital sequencing** — not competence.

| Reality | Implication |
|---|---|
| Full-time Feb 2024; Stoneham end-2024 busier than Boston | Double down on Stoneham first |
| Ultrasound (~2 in MA), regenerative, PDGF, Alo/Derma Clay early | Lead marketing with these |
| Obsessive follow-through | Ordered checklists she can finish |
| Comparison spiral when soft | Scorecard only — no competitor feeds |
| Soft since summer; first YoY down year | 30-day booking recovery before rebrand/spend |
| Rebrand fear = lose Angel Face search | Phased brand system, not hard cutover |
| Wants facials/devices/hyperbaric/staff | Phase 4 — unlock with cash from Phase 1–2 |

**Gate rule:** No hard rebrand cutover and no big-space / device spend until Phase 1 utilization recovers.
